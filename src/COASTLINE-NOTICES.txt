Coastline coordinates

The high-resolution coastline is made with Natural Earth (public domain).
The input is world_110m.txt from the gnuplotting.org map demonstration.
The low-resolution input is world.dat from the same demonstration, identified
in its header as gnuplot world.dat revision 1.2 (2006-06-02). The Dunder
generator quantizes and packs the coordinate data. It omits one endpoint in
the low-resolution series to reproduce the Ratatui 0.30.2 coordinate set.
